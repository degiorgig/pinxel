First release of Pinxel: screen capture and annotation for macOS 15 and later (Apple Silicon and Intel).

## What's new

**Capture**
- Area, window (with or without its shadow) and full screen, each with its own shortcut (defaults ⌃⇧1–3).
- Repeat the last area (⌃⇧4).
- Scrolling capture (⌃⇧5): pick the area and scroll yourself; Pinxel stitches the parts together.
- After capture: editor, quick preview or clipboard only. Captures can also be saved automatically as `.pinxel` or PNG.

**Editor**
- **Tools:** arrows, lines, shapes, text, callouts, numbered steps, pen and highlighter.
- **Effects:** blur, pixelate and magnifier, which act on everything below them.
- **Image:** crop, cut out a band, resize, border, shadow and padding.
- **Objects:** the capture and dropped images are ordinary objects. Click to select, then move, rotate, bring forward or send back (Arrange menu, right-click, toolbar buttons). The canvas always fits the content, in every direction.
- **Paste:** ⌘V replaces the bottom image and keeps the annotations; ⌥⌘V adds an image.
- **Conveniences:** quick styles, smart guides, locked objects, zoom and scrolling on the canvas.

**Library**
- Every capture is saved in `~/Pictures/Pinxel/Libreria`.
- The strip under the editor (⌃⌘L) has folders, tags and search.
- Drag thumbnails onto the canvas, into the Finder or into other apps.

**Export**
- PNG, JPEG, HEIC and PDF, plus sharing and drag and drop.

The app interface is in Italian in this version.

## Install

Open the `.dmg` and drag Pinxel into Applications, or unzip the `.zip`. On first launch, grant the Screen Recording permission.

This build is signed with a development certificate and is not notarized. It opens normally on the Mac of whoever built it. Elsewhere macOS blocks it: open it with right-click ▸ Open.
