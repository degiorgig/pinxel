Pinxel 0.4.2 fixes exporting and the steps that follow a capture.

## Fixes

- Export: the format and scale options in the Export panel respond to clicks, and the scale starts at 1x.
- PDF export: text and arrows drawn above the capture stay sharp and selectable, and anything covered by a later object (text under a filled shape, pixels under an opaque line) is no longer hidden inside the file. Colors match the PNG export.
- Automatic saving runs in the background, so a large capture no longer holds up the next one. If saving fails, Pinxel tells you and opens the capture in the editor.
- Quick previews stay on screen: when they reach the top they start a new column, and they stay on the display where the first one appeared.
- A scrolling capture that stops because of an error now says so, opens what was captured in the editor and leaves it out of the library. Stopping it yourself works as before.

## Install

Open the `.dmg` and drag Pinxel into Applications, or unzip the `.zip`. On first launch, grant the Screen Recording permission.

The app is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally on any Mac.
