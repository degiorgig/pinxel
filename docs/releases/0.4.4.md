Pinxel 0.4.4 makes the capture strip under the editor easier to use.

## Changes

- The capture strip can be resized: drag its top edge to make it taller or shorter, and the thumbnails grow with it. The height is remembered for every window; double-click the edge to go back to the default.
- Thumbnails are no longer cut at the bottom at the default height.
- Hover a thumbnail and click the X in its top right corner to move that capture to the Trash.

## Install

Open the `.dmg` and drag Pinxel into Applications, or unzip the `.zip`. On first launch, grant the Screen Recording permission.

The app is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally on any Mac.
