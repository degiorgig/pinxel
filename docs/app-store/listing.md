## English (U.S.)

**Name:** Pinxel
**Subtitle:** Screen capture and annotation

**Promotional text:**
Capture an area, a window, the full screen or a whole scrolling page, then annotate it with arrows, text, steps and blur.

**Keywords:** screenshot,capture,annotate,markup,scrolling,arrow,blur,highlight,editor,screen

**Description:**
Pinxel captures your screen and lets you explain it in seconds.

CAPTURE
• An area, a window (with or without its shadow) or the full screen
• The last area again, with one shortcut
• Scrolling capture: scroll a long page and Pinxel stitches it into one image
• After each capture, open the editor, show a quick preview or just copy it to the clipboard

ANNOTATE
• Arrows, lines, shapes, text, callouts and numbered steps
• Pen and highlighter
• Blur and pixelate to hide private details
• Magnifier to zoom in on a detail
• Crop, cut out a band, resize, add a border, shadow and padding
• Quick styles, smart guides, locked objects, unlimited undo

ORGANIZE AND SHARE
• Every capture goes into the library, with folders, tags and search
• Export to PNG, JPEG, HEIC or PDF, share it, or drag it into another app
• .pinxel documents keep annotations editable

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Pinxel works entirely on your Mac. It does not connect to the internet and collects no data.

Free for personal and other noncommercial use, including schools, charities and public institutions. Companies and other commercial use need a license: see the License Agreement.

## Review notes (App Review Information)

Pinxel is a menu bar app (no Dock icon until a window opens). To try it: click the Pinxel icon in the menu bar
and choose a capture, or press ⌃⇧1 (area). The Screen Recording permission is needed only to take the
captures the user starts; captures never leave the Mac. Scrolling capture (⌃⇧5): select an area over a long
page, scroll it, then press Return or click Done.
