## English (U.S.)

**Name:** Pinxel
**Subtitle:** Captures you can keep editing

**Promotional text:**
Combine several captures and images on one canvas, keep every annotation editable, and find any capture again in the library. Scrolling pages included.

**Keywords:** screenshot,capture,annotate,markup,scrolling,compose,library,blur,editor,screen

**Description:**
Pinxel turns screen captures into documents you can keep working on. Capture, compose several images on one canvas, explain them with annotations, and reopen them weeks later to change anything.

A CANVAS, NOT A FLAT IMAGE
• The capture is an object like any other: move it, rotate it, bring it forward or send it back
• Drop more captures or images onto the same canvas to compare or combine them
• The canvas grows and shrinks to fit the content, so nothing gets cut off

DOCUMENTS THAT STAY EDITABLE
• .pinxel documents keep every arrow, text and blur editable, and the original images untouched
• Open a capture again at any time and change, move or remove any annotation
• Export a flat copy to PNG, JPEG, HEIC or PDF only when you need one

A LIBRARY FOR EVERY CAPTURE
• Every capture is kept in the library, in a strip right under the editor (⌃⌘L)
• Folders, tags and search to find it again
• Drag a capture from the library into the editor or into another app

CAPTURE ANYTHING
• An area, a window (with or without its shadow) or the full screen
• The last area again, with one shortcut
• Scrolling capture: scroll a long page and Pinxel stitches it into one image, with no Accessibility permission
• After each capture, open the editor, show a quick preview or just copy it to the clipboard

EXPLAIN IT CLEARLY
• Arrows, lines, shapes, text, callouts and numbered steps
• Pen and highlighter, magnifier for a detail
• Blur and pixelate to hide private details
• Crop, cut out a band, resize, add a border, shadow and padding
• Quick styles, smart guides, locked objects, unlimited undo

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Pinxel works entirely on your Mac. It does not connect to the internet and collects no data.

One purchase covers personal and work use on all your Macs. No subscription, no account.

## Review notes (App Review Information)

Pinxel is a menu bar app (no Dock icon until a window opens). To try it: click the Pinxel icon in the menu bar
and choose a capture, or press ⌃⇧1 (area). The Screen Recording permission is needed only to take the
captures the user starts; captures never leave the Mac. Scrolling capture (⌃⇧5): select an area over a long
page, scroll it, then press Return or click Done. On first launch Pinxel asks once whether to open at login; it
stays off unless the user says yes, and can be changed in Settings ▸ General. Pinxel asks for no folder access of its own: the library lives in
the app container, and automatic saving writes only to a folder the user picks in the standard Open panel
(Settings ▸ Automatic Saving ▸ Choose…).
