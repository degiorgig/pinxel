# Pinxel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Pinxel is a screen capture and annotation app for macOS. It does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- **Captures stay on your Mac.** Screenshots, annotations and documents are saved only where you choose: the Pinxel library in `~/Pictures/Pinxel/Library`, the folders you pick, or the clipboard.
- **No network access.** Pinxel does not connect to the internet, and has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- **Permissions.** Pinxel asks for the Screen Recording permission only to take the captures you start. It uses the permission for nothing else.
- **Settings** are stored locally in macOS user defaults.

When you share a capture or drag it into another app, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/degiorgig/pinxel/issues>.
